# Background

Sonika Saggi is a Financial Advisor based out of Surrey, BC, and has worked full-time in the life insurance and financial services industry for the past 20 years. Sonika approached [Q5](https://q-5.ca) with the desire to revitalize her digital presence and attract clients in ways above and beyond traditional word-of-mouth. In particular, she wanted to expand her customer base to include younger families who had grown up with the internet and had a keener eye for the quality of a brand's digital presence.

# Role

While I work with my partner during most phases of the project, my focus is, of course, on implementation.

    All of Q5's projects begin with a discovery meeting to review the current
    site and its major pain points. Outdated design, confusing information
    hierarchy, and broken pages were quickly identified. We proposed a simpler
    website with a fresh new brand, prioritizing faster load times, easy-to-use
    interactions, and a seamless mobile experience.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="leaf" title="2. Brand design">
    For younger families, the brand needed to communicate security, growth, and
    warmth while staying modern and minimal. The logotype pairs a simple
    sans-serif with a bright green leaf, and a subtle gradient across all visual
    elements adds consistency and a sense of motion, even in print.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="message-circle" title="3. Interaction design">
    The site's goal is to start a conversation. A friendly contact form offers
    visitors a free quote, walking them through one simple step at a time and
    asking only for what Sonika needs. Completed forms are emailed straight to
    her.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="code" title="4. Implementation">
    The site is built on Craft CMS, my content management system of choice. It
    let us iterate quickly and gives Sonika an intuitive interface to update
    her own content, minimizing the need for recurring services.
  </Card>
</Cards>

# Results

Through updated modern design, Sonika now has a website and brand that reflects her state-of-the-art services.

## Lighthouse results

Using modern web development methods, we made measurable improvements to sonikasaggi.com's Google Lighthouse score. This score is based on the website's performance, accessibility, and use of best practices and is an important factor in the site's search ranking.
